# Digital Heroes: Player Manual 📖

Welcome to **Digital Heroes**! We are thrilled to have you join our community where your love for golf directly contributes to incredible charities, while giving you the chance to win massive cash prizes every single month.

This guide will walk you through exactly how to set up your profile, log your scores, and ensure you are eligible for the next big draw!

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Creating Your Account
1. Visit the Digital Heroes homepage and click **Join Now**.
2. To create your account, provide your standard details: Email, Full Name, and a secure password.
3. **The Most Important Step**: Choose your charity! You'll see a dropdown of verified local and national charities. Select the one closest to your heart. 
4. Complete your subscription. You can choose a **Monthly (£19.99)** or **Yearly (£199.00 - Best Value)** plan. 

*Note: The moment your subscription is active, 10% of your fee is instantly locked in to be sent directly to your chosen charity.*

---

## 2. The Dashboard

Once subscribed, you will be taken to your **Golfer Dashboard**. This is your command centre.

At the top of your dashboard, you will see your live metrics:
* **Status**: Confirms your subscription is Active.
* **Winnings**: Your total accumulated prize money from drawing matches.
* **Impact**: The total amount of money you have personally raised for your chosen charity.

### Your Lucky Numbers
In the bottom right corner of your dashboard, you will see a dark green card labeled **Upcoming Draw**. This shows your 5 "Lucky Numbers" for the next month's draw. If you haven't played golf yet, these will display as `?`.

---

## 3. Entering The Draw (Logging Scores)

Digital Heroes uses your actual golf performance as your lottery numbers. 

### How to get your 5 numbers:
Every time you play a round of golf, you need to log your **Stableford Score** (a standard scoring system in golf typically ranging between 1 and 45).

1. Click the gold **"+ Add New Score"** button on your dashboard.
2. Enter the date you played the round.
3. Enter your final Stableford score.
4. Click **Save Score**.

### The Rolling "5-Score" Rule
You need exactly **5 scores** to enter the draw. 
If you play *more* than 5 times in a month, don't worry! The system uses a "Rolling Average" mechanic. The moment you input a 6th score, your oldest score automatically drops off, ensuring your 5 most recent rounds are always acting as your draw numbers.

---

## 4. The Monthly Draw & Winnings

On the **15th of every month**, the Digital Heroes Draw Engine kicks into gear. 

The system will automatically randomly generate 5 numbers between 1 and 45.

### Prize Verification
If your 5 logged scores match the draw numbers, you win!
* **Match 5:** You win the Jackpot! (40% of the entire global prize pool)
* **Match 4:** You win the Runner Up Tier.
* **Match 3:** You win a standard payout.

**IMPORTANT: Keep your scorecards!**
If you win a major prize (Match 4 or 5), the Digital Heroes Admin team will require you to upload a photograph of your signed scorecards from those specific dates to verify the scores were legitimate before the cash is released to your bank account.
